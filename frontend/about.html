About FoxScanner

FoxScanner reads a fresh BNB memecoin and shows who holds it: linked wallets, snipers, fresh wallets, and how hard the price could fall.

Origins

Built for tokens that launch on BNB Chain and move before anyone has time to read the wallets. Paste an address, and the fox reads the transfers.

Mission

Collect the launch, the transfers, and the holder wallets, then turn them into one verdict. four.meme, Flap, and Genius.fun go through the same check.

Commitment

Every token is scored the same way. The verdict is the holder data. It is not adjusted for a project or a payment.

How a scan works

One score. Every token.

Linked wallets, snipers, fresh wallets, and how concentrated the supply is. That is the check. Contract owner, mint, honeypot, and tax sit outside this scan.
