Trust boundary

Verification proves control, not safety.

What it proves

A wallet completed the required signature and can maintain the published token identity.

What it does not prove

It does not guarantee price, liquidity, contract safety, or honest future behavior.

Score independence

Payment and verification never improve the holder-risk score. Evidence determines the verdict.
